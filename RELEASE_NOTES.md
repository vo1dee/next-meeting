# Next Meeting 1.0.0

Next Meeting is now ready for its first public release.

Connect your Google Calendar account, then use the Next Meeting key to see the time remaining until your next meeting and join it with one press. The Agenda Dial lets you browse today's eligible meetings and join the selected one.

The plugin requests read-only calendar access and performs OAuth directly on your computer using PKCE. Calendar credentials and data are not sent to a Next Meeting server.

See the [privacy policy](docs/privacy-policy.md) for data-handling details.
